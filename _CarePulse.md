**Title:** *CarePulse*: Telehealth & Smart Scheduling Platform 

*CarePulse: Telehealth & Smart Scheduling Platform is a web-based healthcare management portal. It bridges the gap between patients and healthcare providers by combining online virtual consultations with an automated, conflict-free appointment scheduling system.* 

*Objectives*

1. **Streamline Appointment Booking:** Automate the scheduling process to eliminate overlapping bookings, reduce patient wait times, and remove manual scheduling errors.   
2. **Expand Healthcare Accessibility:** Enable patients—especially those in remote areas or with mobility constraints—to consult with certified doctors via secure telehealth options.   
3. **Digitize Medical Records & Prescriptions:** Provide a centralized dashboard where patients can access visit histories, doctor recommendations, and digital prescriptions in one secure place.   
4. **Optimize Clinic Operations:** Equip doctors and administrative staff with real-time schedule management, queue status, and patient records access. 

*Features by User Role* 

**A. Patient Module** 

* **Smart Booking Engine:** Select medical specialties, view doctor availability slots in real time, and pick an available time slot.   
* **Telehealth Consultation Room:** In-app video/audio link or secure meeting integration for virtual appointments.   
* **Digital Medical History:** Access past doctor notes, visit logs, and downloadable digital prescriptions.   
* **Automated Notifications:** SMS or email reminders for upcoming appointments and status updates (Pending, Confirmed, Cancelled). 

### **B. Doctor Module**

* **Schedule & Availability Control:** Define working hours, mark break times, and accept or reschedule incoming requests.  
* **Patient Record Viewer:** Review a patient's past visit logs before starting a session.  
* **E-Prescription & Consultation Notes:** Input diagnosis details and generate digital prescription forms post-consultation.

### **C. Admin Module**

* **User & Staff Management:** Manage accounts for doctors, patients, and administrative staff.  
* **Clinic Queue & Status Overview:** Track daily appointment counts, room assignments, and consultation statuses.  
* **System Analytics:** Monitor key operational metrics such as total completed consultations, peak booking times, and cancellation rates.

## *Problems CarePulse Solves*

* **Long Waiting Times & Overcrowded Clinics:** Replaces long, physically exhausting clinic queues with structured, pre-scheduled time slots**.**  
* **Double Bookings & Scheduling Conflicts:** Prevents overlapping slots through automated real-time calendar locks.  
* **Geographic Barriers:** Eliminates travel hurdles for basic non-emergency follow-ups, minor check-ups, or medical advice via telehealth visits.  
* **Lost Medical Papers:** Replaces physical paper records and hand-written scripts with digital, searchable storage.


*Pros and Cons of the System* 

| Pros | Cons |
| ----- | ----- |
| **24/7 Accessibility:** Patients can book appointments at any time without calling clinic staff. | **Internet Dependency:** Requires stable internet connections for real-time booking and video calls. |
| **Operational Efficiency:** Reduces administrative workload on clinic staff. | **Limited Physical Examination:** Cannot fully replace physical check-ups, palpation, or urgent emergency care. |
| **Centralized Data:** Keeps prescriptions, appointment history, and medical records in one organized portal. | **Data Privacy & Security Overhead:** Requires strict security measures (e.g., encryption, access controls) to protect sensitive medical records. |
| **Reduced No-Shows:** Automated reminders keep patients notified of scheduled visits. | **Digital Literacy Barrier:** Elderly or non-tech-savvy users may require assistance navigating the portal. |
